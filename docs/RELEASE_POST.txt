Title
Video Sampler for Fingersonic Beats v0.4.6 | Play Video with Your Drum Pads

Introduction
Turn local video clips into a playable instrument. Assign a video to each of ten Fingersonic Beats pads, choose its clip range, and trigger picture and sound with MIDI. Audio goes directly into Logic Pro for recording, bouncing, and mixing.

Features
• Ten independently assigned video pads.
• Per-pad clip trimming, MIDI Learn, manual note mapping, and velocity response.
• Live multiview video playback and up to 16 audio voices.
• Pad settings saved with the Logic project; keep the original videos available separately.
• macOS 13.0 or later, with Apple Silicon and Intel binaries included.

Install and use
This is an AUv3 instrument installed with the complete Video Sampler.app. Drag the app into Applications, approve its first launch if needed, then load it in Logic:
AU Instruments → Fingersonic → Video Sampler → Stereo

First launch
This test build uses ad-hoc signing and is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, try opening the app, then use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway after checking the source. A complete guide is included.

New in v0.4.6
A Fingersonic family video-sampler icon, support for discovering Logic Pro Creator Studio from the Open Logic Pro button, and complete English installation and usage documentation.

Downloads

• VideoSampler-0.4.6-Complete.zip: both of the above, plus publishing material, verification notes, and a file-integrity manifest.

Compatibility and workflow
Logic Pro is the primary host; other AUv3 instrument hosts have not been individually verified. No AUv2 .component, VST3, Windows, or iOS package is included. Offline bounce exports audio. The plug-in does not export a combined video file; use a separate screen-recording workflow for a performance video.
